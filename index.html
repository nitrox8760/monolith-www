The mess

Compliance shouldn’t live in WhatsApp and half-finished spreadsheets.

Most teams still stitch visits together after the fact. Due dates slip. Duration tests get “cleared” without a real discharge. Defects vanish into last month’s PDF.

  • Paper logbooks and Excel that never match what’s on the wall
  • Photos and notes scattered across phones with no site register
  • Annual due wiped without timing every test switch

See Beacon

Field for the visit. Console for the office.

One organisation, one sync backend. Technicians work offline in the Field PWA. Managers oversee sites, history, team, and certificates on desktop.

How it works

Three steps from empty site to issued paperwork.

  1. 1

    Add the site

    Create a site or import fittings from a logbook spreadsheet. Set switches, Site IDs, and tracking.

  2. 2

    Run the test

    Monthly function or timed annual by test switch — offline in the Field app, with results as you go.

  3. 3

    Issue & sync

    Download the visit PIR or site periodic certificate. Sync when you’re back online. Due dates stay honest.

Why Beacon

Built for how emergency lighting is actually tested.

  • Offline-first Plant rooms and basements included — sync when you’re back online.
  • Timed annual by test switch Real discharge timers. No clearing due without finishing the switches.
  • Living asset register Site IDs, fittings, and switches that carry forward visit after visit.
  • Paperwork that matches the work Test report, visit PIR, and periodic certificate — immutable once issued.
  • Open defects on the site Failures become a register, not a note buried in last month’s PDF.
