    FAQ

    The questions we’d ask before trying it too.

    Does it work when there’s no signal?

    Yes. Field is built for plant rooms and basements. Keep testing with no bars; when you’re back online, your work catches up. Console is for the office when you’ve got a connection.

    Is this enough for BS 5266?

    Beacon is built around how emergency lighting is actually tested: monthly and annual runs, a proper site register, open defects, visit reports, and periodic certificates. It helps you record the work and issue the paperwork — the duty-holder stays responsible for compliance. It isn’t a BSI product certification.

    What’s Field vs Console?

    Field is the phone or tablet app you take on site — testing, defects, and history, even offline. Console is the desktop side for sites, team, history, and certificates across the company. Same world, two jobs.

    Can I bring in an existing logbook?

    Yes. Add a site, import your fittings from a spreadsheet, then set test switches and site IDs before the first visit. No need to retype the building from scratch.

    Do I need an App Store download?

    No. Open Field in the browser on your phone or tablet and add it to your home screen. Console is just a normal desktop browser. No card needed to start the open beta.

    What paperwork can I issue today?

    After a visit: a fitting test report and a visit periodic inspection report. For the site: a periodic inspection and testing certificate. Once something is issued and locked, it stays as signed.

    How much does it cost?

    Free in open beta. We’ll tell you about pricing before anything is charged — no quiet lock-in.

    Where is my data kept?

    Your organisation’s data lives securely in Beacon’s cloud. On site, Field can keep the visit on the phone until you’re back online. Details are on the privacy page.
