Questions about the templates

Are these free to download?

Yes. Both PDFs are free, with no signup. They are blank forms for you to fill in — not certificates issued by Beacon.

What is the difference between the visit report and the site certificate?

The visit report is what you hand over after a test. The site certificate is the record for the building, kept with the emergency lighting logbook — often alongside those visit reports. Full guide: visit report vs certificate vs logbook. What each logbook entry needs: emergency lighting logbook (UK).

Do these meet BS 5266?

They follow the same layout as Beacon’s BS 5266 paperwork, so the fields your clients expect are there. You and the duty-holder stay responsible for the work itself.
